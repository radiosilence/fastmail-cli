- **Mailing lists, unsubscribing and bulk sweeps.** `mailingLists` (and `fastmail lists`) groups every email carrying `List-Unsubscribe` by sender, with counts, unread counts and the newest unsubscribe links; `unsubscribe` (and `fastmail unsubscribe`) leaves a list by RFC 8058 one-click POST, restricted to public HTTPS addresses with no redirects, or by its `mailto:` address, PREVIEW then CONFIRM; `Email.unsubscribe` parses the headers on any email; `sweepEmails` moves, marks read or marks spam everything a filter matches; `markAsNotSpam` (and `fastmail not-spam`) undoes a spam verdict. Spam verdicts now set `$junk`/`$notjunk` as well as moving the email. Fastmail's filter rules are not reachable over JMAP, so they are not managed here.
